Company overview

Thoughtful sourcing for a connected food world.

ZAPAX is an India-based sourcing and export partner focused on premium dehydrated agricultural ingredients. We connect international buyers with producing partners and coordinate buyer-led specifications, packaging discussions and export planning.

Our work begins with understanding what an importer needs to deliver to their own customers. Product availability, origin, processing and exact specifications are confirmed for each inquiry and order.

Who we are

Founded in Limbdi. Motivated by a bigger idea.

Chetan Punani and Keval Chauhan founded ZAPAX in Limbdi, Gujarat, with the motivation to build a trusted bridge between buyers and producing partners. They know export orders carry more than product: they carry timelines, documents, commercial expectations and the confidence of a buyer’s customers.

We are not a manufacturer. We are a sourcing and export partner that values careful listening, clear communication and steady follow-through at every stage of a trade relationship.

Our mission

Make sourcing clearer, more human and easier to coordinate.

We aim to connect buyers with suitable producing partners, bring detail to product discussions and support the practical coordination that helps an inquiry move toward a well-understood order.

Our vision

Grow into a trusted partner across products and markets.

Our ambition is to grow ZAPAX through responsible relationships, learn from each buyer brief and explore new import and export opportunities as market needs and dependable partners evolve.
