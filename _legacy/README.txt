ZAPAX WEBSITE PROJECT
=====================

Pages and local assets
- index.html: responsive sourcing and product homepage.
- about.html: company overview, mission, vision, services, workflow and core values.
- styles.css / app.js: shared responsive styles and navigation, loader, product filters, scroll-to-top and inquiry interactions.
- zapax-logo.png / zapax-logo.svg: supplied ZAPAX wordmark and the SVG loader version.
- produce-hero.svg and product category SVG files: local artwork without remote image requests.
- ZAPAX-product-overview.pdf: buyer-facing product overview.
- robots.txt and sitemap.xml: crawler files for the assumed www.zapax.com domain.

Open index.html in a browser. Keep the files together.

Before publishing
1. Confirm the live domain and update canonical links, metadata, schema, robots.txt and sitemap.xml if it differs from www.zapax.com.
2. Add the verified company email and phone number, set the inquiry email recipient or connect the form to a backend/CRM.
3. Replace social search links with official company profile URLs once confirmed.
4. Confirm the registered legal company name and business address before replacing the current brand descriptor.
5. Replace the clearly labeled illustrative testimonials with genuine, customer-approved feedback before publication.
6. The policy URLs point to the aethexhealthcare.com pages supplied for this brief. Replace them with ZAPAX-approved policies if those are not the intended legal documents.
